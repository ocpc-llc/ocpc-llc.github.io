Registration & fees

Onsite participation in Hong Kong is free for all teams taking part in person. Teams competing remotely on the Hong Kong schedule pay the regular participation fee under the usual rules — see fees & discounts on the home page. Onsite capacity is limited, and applications for onsite participation are processed on a first-come-first-served basis, so please register early.

Register using the registration form (pick "Hong Kong — onsite", or remote participation following the Hong Kong schedule). Join our Discord or contact us with any questions. Registration closes 25 July 2026 for onsite teams and 1 August 2026 for remote teams; the early-bird fee applies until 8 July 2026.

The early-bird / hardship / Ukraine discounts and what the fee covers are the same across all sites — see registration & participation fee on the home page. Payments are processed by OCPC LLC, the operator of ocpc.camp — by registering you agree to the Terms & Refund Policy.
